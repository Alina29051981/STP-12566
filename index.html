About the Game

These aren't your grandma's match-3 puzzles

Trailer Park Boys Match delivers a match-3 experience with a strong comedic twist. Each level feels lively and unpredictable, with visual humor and character-driven moments. As you get better at the puzzles, you'll unlock more of the park's shenanigans and see what the boys are up to.
